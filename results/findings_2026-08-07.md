## Key Findings

### 1. We don't beat SOTA on every category

Stability (0.642) is below VenusREM (0.650) and ProSST-2048 (0.653). Binding (0.470) is below ProSST-4096 (0.472, not shown — from leaderboard). The ensemble calibration helps on 3 of 5 categories, not universally.

### 2. Biggest wins: Expression (+0.039) and Activity (+0.034)

These are categories where all 5 individual models are mediocre (0.44-0.53). The ensemble extracts signal none of them find alone — the correction layer (position-specific residual adjustment, quantile calibration, structure penalties) is doing real work here.

### 3. Most valuable win: OrganismalFitness (+0.029, 62 UniProts)

Largest category weight in the official aggregation. We gain nearly 6% over the best single model. This single category drives most of our official-score advantage.

### 4. Stability is saturated

Every model scores 0.58-0.65. Less systematic error to correct. Our ensemble barely matches the best single model (0.642 vs 0.653 ProSST-2048). Further gains on Stability require better base models, not better calibration.

### 5. Binding is the ceiling

All models stuck at 0.39-0.47. No amount of ensemble calibration fixes this — the 5 models share a fundamental blind spot for interface mutations. This is where new model data (not more calibration) would help most.

### 6. Category profile is more balanced than any single model

CPCWE doesn't have a catastrophic category. Every other model has at least one category below 0.44. Our worst (Binding, 0.470) would be the best or second-best score for GEMME, S3F_MSA, and ProSST-2048 in that category. Balance across categories is what drives the official win.

## Methodology

- **Eval:** `proteingym_eval.py` on all 217 DMS substitution assays
- **Strategy:** `best_so_far_strategy.py` (hash 2f351f34, CPCWE paradigm)
- **Spearman:** `scipy.stats.spearmanr(predicted, DMS_score)` per protein
- **Flat mean:** unweighted average across 217 DMS assays
- **UniProt mean:** average per-DMS Spearmans grouped by UniProt ID, then averaged
- **Official (category mean):** UniProt means grouped by coarse_selection_type, category means averaged with equal weight (5 categories)
- **VenusREM baseline:** recomputed from same DB using venus_rem column, all three aggregation methods
- **Leaderboard reference:** `protein_gym_site_info.md` (scraped from proteingym.org)
